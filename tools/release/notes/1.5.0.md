Amount format setting

- New **More → Settings** screen with an **Amount format** option:
  Czech `9 133,58 $` (default, unchanged), US `$9,133.58` or European `9.133,58 $`.
  The change applies across the whole app immediately.
- Amount fields accept thousands separators (`9,133.58`, `9.133,58`, `9 133,58`).
- The chosen format is saved in backups and restored with them.
